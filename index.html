Job Application Assistant

Job Application Assistant is a desktop application that helps users send job application emails through their own Gmail account using Google OAuth.

The app only sends emails explicitly initiated by the user. No data is stored externally and no background emails are sent without user action. 📧 JOB APPLICATION ASSISTANT ============================= Version: 1.0.0 Developer: RiDDL3TeCH (Ephantus Mwaura - Kenya) Email: snaidermwaura104@gmail.com 🚀 FEATURES: ------------ 1. You CANNOT use your regular Gmail password! Google requires a special "App Password": To Get Gmail App Password: Go to: https://myaccount.google.com/security Turn on "2-Step Verification" (if not already on) Go to: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords Select "Mail" → "Other" → Name it "Job App Assistant" Copy the 16-character password shown Use this password in the Settings tab 📁 Where Your Data Goes Your information is saved in: text C:\Users\YOURNAME\JobApplicationAssistant\ recipients.json - Your contact list settings.json - Your email settings history.csv - Records of sent emails Everything stays on your computer only. 💡 Quick Tips for Beginners Starting Fresh: Go to Settings tab → Enter your email Get App Password (for Gmail) Test connection Add companies in Recipients tab Write your email template Add your resume Send!
